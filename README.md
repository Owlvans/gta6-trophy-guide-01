Leonida Trophy Guide — an offline trophy tracker and platinum roadmap for Grand Theft Auto VI. All 55 trophies with their official names, descriptions and grades; a cited guide for each one, written in the guide's own words, now with a "When it unlocks" line and a "Pitfalls" line for every trophy; editable counters for every tracked target (the 42 Shooting Range golds, the 500 choices, the $250,000 laundering total and 18 more); personal notes per trophy; a screened missable list (one potential missable, flagged with its Chapter 1 window); a spoiler shield that hides story trophies until they are earned; a roadmap with per-stage progress bars and effort notes; a platinum plan panel; and a Next-up planner that lists what to earn next, in roadmap order, from your own progress. Progress, counters and notes save in the browser, with export and import. No account, no network — the app runs entirely from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 — the app is fully offline
```
